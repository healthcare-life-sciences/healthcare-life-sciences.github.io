About The Event

The Healthcare and Life Sciences Symposium is co-located with the 2025 Knowledge Graph Conference.

The symposium will cover topics around data integration, data profiling, data curation, querying, knowledge discovery, ontology mapping, matching, reconciliation, machine learning / generative AI approaches, and applications.
